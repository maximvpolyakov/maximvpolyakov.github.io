Profile

About

I am an Associate Professor and Senior Research Fellow at Volgograd State University. My research is centred on mathematical modelling and numerical simulation, especially their applications to biological systems and problems in oncology.

My current research investigates the spatiotemporal dynamics of solid tumours during CAR-T cell therapy. I am interested in how antigen escape, immune-cell infiltration, exhaustion and the tumour microenvironment influence the outcome of treatment, and how mechanistic models can be combined with machine learning to make their parameters more interpretable.

I received my Candidate of Technical Sciences degree (PhD equivalent) in 2023, specialising in mathematical modelling, numerical methods and software systems. My earlier research focused on thermal processes in biological tissues and computational approaches to medical diagnostics.

Focus

Research

I study how mathematical models can explain the behaviour of complex biological systems and help distinguish the mechanisms behind different outcomes.

CAR-T cell therapy in solid tumours

Mechanistic models of tumour–immune interactions, spatial heterogeneity, antigen escape and immunosuppression. A central question is how these mechanisms give rise to distinct therapeutic response regimes.

Reaction–diffusion models · Chemotaxis · Spatiotemporal simulation

Mechanistic and data-driven modelling

Combining differential-equation models with machine learning, parameter inference and numerical analysis to investigate latent biological mechanisms and improve interpretability under data constraints.

Hybrid ML · Parameter inference · Computational methods

Mathematical modelling in medicine

Computational studies of heat transfer, temperature and radiation fields in biological tissues, and their applications to microwave radiometry and computer-assisted medical diagnostics.

Bioheat transfer · Inverse problems · Numerical simulation
